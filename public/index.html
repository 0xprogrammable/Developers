02 / Integration contract

Integrate once. We carry the changes.

Custom activation and future compatible deployments flow through the same versioned interface.

What remains stable

  • Envelope Launch identity, category, chain, token, verification and capabilities.
  • Categories Public launches remain either classic or custom.
  • Evolution New v1 fields are optional and additive. Breaking changes require a new major version.

What your integration does

  • Discover Read deployment sources from the manifest rather than hardcoding addresses.
  • Verify Preserve launch proof and verification state alongside each record.
  • Extend Ignore unknown optional fields and gate rich behavior by declared capabilities.

Existing first-party stock-paired records normalize as Custom. The open community Custom registry remains prelaunch.
